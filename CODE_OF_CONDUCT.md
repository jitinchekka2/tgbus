# Code of conduct

This project is meant to be a friendly, useful place for people who care about
public transport data. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith, including toward newcomers;
- give and accept constructive feedback about work, not people;
- refrain from harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome
  sexual attention;
- not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may ban people who
break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private "Report a
vulnerability" / contact options on the repository, or reach the maintainer
through their GitHub profile.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
